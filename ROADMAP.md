# Roadmap

**Next**
- Test and tune on a physical Quest 3: tray height, pinch thresholds, passthrough lighting.
- Snap furniture to real floors and walls with WebXR plane detection and hit-test.

**Then**
- Bring a room scanned with a phone in the ReRoom web studio into the headset.
- Generate a new piece in the headset from a spoken description or a product photo.
- Share a furnished room as a link others open on their own Quest.

**Later**
- Retailer mode: a store's catalogue at true size in a customer's living room.
